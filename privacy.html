Privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This policy describes Hermes Personal, a Google Workspace integration operated by Moldowin for personal use. This informational website is not a public hosted agent or a service accepting other users’ Google accounts.

Google data accessed and why

  • Gmail: message metadata, message contents and attachments when needed for requested email tasks; permissions also allow sending approved messages and modifying labels.
  • Calendar: calendars and event details for scheduling and approved event management.
  • Drive, Docs and Sheets: file metadata and requested file, document or spreadsheet contents for search, summaries, and approved file operations or edits.
  • Contacts: contact information for requested lookups; Contacts access is read-only.

Access is limited to the account and scopes approved through Google OAuth. The integration does not collect the Google account password. Data is used to provide the operator’s requested personal-assistant functionality, not for advertising, sale, or data brokerage.

Processing and transfers

Hermes accesses Google APIs from operator-controlled devices. Relevant data may be included in prompts processed by the language-model providers configured in Hermes, or delivered to private destinations explicitly configured or requested by the operator. Such processing is limited to delivering the requested functionality. Data is not made public by this website.

The integration does not retain Google Workspace data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized AI or machine-learning models. Before transferring Google data to a model provider, the operator must use provider terms and account settings that prohibit that training use; a provider’s retention and data-control settings must be reviewed rather than assumed.

Hermes Personal’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Third-party sharing is limited to necessary requested functionality, security, applicable legal requirements, or other transfers permitted by that policy.

Storage, security and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on operator-controlled devices with access restricted to the operating-system account. Credentials are not committed to this public repository. Google data and generated responses may remain in private conversation history, task outputs, logs, archives or backups until the operator deletes them under the applicable retention settings.

Model-provider operational retention depends on the selected provider’s terms and settings. This policy does not claim zero retention, exclusively local processing, encryption of every stored file, or automatic deletion after each request.

Revocation and deletion

The operator can revoke Google API access at Google Account connections and remove the local integration credentials. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase existing conversations, downloaded files, model-provider records, or backups. Those records must be deleted through the relevant application, storage system, or provider controls.

This public website

The site has no application-level analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, forms, or Google OAuth callback endpoint. GitHub hosts it and may process ordinary visitor information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The public repository contains only the site’s informational content.

Contact and changes

Contact the operator through GitHub issues for non-sensitive questions. Do not post private data or credentials there. This notice will be updated if the integration’s purpose or data handling changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
